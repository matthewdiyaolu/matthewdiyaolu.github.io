About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Arkansas, Sam M. Walton College of Business. I work in applied microeconomics, with a focus on development and labor economics.

My research asks how legal rules, public programs, and the information environment inside households shape investment in people: schooling, health spending, the timing of marriage, and the transition from school to work. I study these questions using natural experiments in Nigeria and Indonesia, and I also run incentivized laboratory experiments on how moral framing changes choices that create congestion and environmental costs.

Before graduate school I spent seven years in the Nigerian insurance industry, most recently as Senior Manager for Reinsurance and Data Analysis at Ark Insurance Brokers. I am a Chartered Property Casualty Underwriter (CPCU) and an Associate Chartered Accountant (ICAN).

My advisor is Arya Gaduh.
